# Design Analysis Inputs
## Purpose
This document defines the required prerequisites before starting the design analysis workflow. It ensures that engineers have all necessary materials to perform a complete review.

## Principles
- **Prerequisite Completion**: Do not start analysis without the required inputs.
- **Source of Truth**: Rely on provided design files and briefs as the absolute truth.

## Engineering Rules
- **Rule 1**: Require access to the final design files (e.g., Figma, Sketch).
- **Rule 2**: Require the completed Engineering Brief from Project Intake.
- **Rule 3**: Require any available brand guidelines or asset libraries.
- **Rule 4**: Require specific details about target devices and supported browsers.

## Best Practices
- Verify access permissions to design tools before scheduling the analysis.
- Review the Engineering Brief to understand the project context.
- Ask for design source files rather than exported images.

## Common Mistakes
- ❌ Starting analysis on draft designs.
- ❌ Not requesting brand guidelines, leading to inconsistent styling.
- ❌ Assuming browser support requirements without confirmation.
- ❌ Analyzing a design without reading the project brief first.

# Design Analysis Process
## Purpose
This document standardizes the step-by-step procedure for analyzing design files. It ensures a consistent, repeatable approach to breaking down designs into engineering requirements.

## Principles
- **Systematic Approach**: Follow a structured path from global to specific.
- **Thorough Documentation**: Record findings as they are discovered.
- **Proactive Questioning**: Formulate questions for designers early.

## Engineering Rules
- **Step 1**: Export and organize all design assets.
- **Step 2**: Extract global design tokens (colors, typography, spacing).
- **Step 3**: Identify and document the layout grid and container sizes.
- **Step 4**: Break down each page into distinct sections.
- **Step 5**: Identify recurring components across sections and pages.
- **Step 6**: Document interactive elements and their states.
- **Step 7**: Compile a list of questions and missing assets for the design team.

## Best Practices
- Use a dedicated tool or spreadsheet for logging tokens and components.
- Keep the design files open alongside the documentation.
- Take screenshots of complex areas for reference.

## Common Mistakes
- ❌ Analyzing pages in isolation rather than looking for global patterns.
- ❌ Failing to document exact pixel values for spacing.
- ❌ Skipping over complex sections assuming they will be figured out later.
- ❌ Not reviewing the design against the original project requirements.

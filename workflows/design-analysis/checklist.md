# Design Analysis Checklist
## Purpose
This document provides a mandatory checklist to ensure all critical aspects of design analysis are completed. It acts as a final verification before moving to the next workflow stage.

## Principles
- **Verification**: Ensure no step in the analysis process was skipped.
- **Accountability**: Provide a clear record that the analysis meets standards.
- **Completeness**: Guarantee all required outputs are ready.

## Engineering Rules
- **Rule 1**: Verify all global colors are documented.
- **Rule 2**: Verify all typography styles are documented.
- **Rule 3**: Verify all spacing tokens are documented.
- **Rule 4**: Verify all breakpoints are analyzed.
- **Rule 5**: Verify all interactive states (hover, focus, active, disabled) are documented.
- **Rule 6**: Verify all assets (images, icons) are identified and exportable.
- **Rule 7**: Verify the component inventory is complete.
- **Rule 8**: Verify all designer questions are compiled into a report.

## Best Practices
- Complete the checklist systematically.
- Do not check off an item unless the corresponding documentation is complete.
- Review the checklist with a peer if dealing with a complex project.

## Common Mistakes
- ❌ Checking items off from memory rather than verifying the documentation.
- ❌ Treating the checklist as a suggestion rather than a mandatory requirement.
- ❌ Skipping asset verification, leading to delays during implementation.
- ❌ Ignoring missing states and assuming they can be improvised.

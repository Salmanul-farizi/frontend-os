# Experience Analysis Inputs
## Purpose
This document defines the required prerequisites before starting the experience analysis. It ensures that the analysis is based on complete design information and technical constraints.

## Principles
- **Prerequisite Completion**: Do not start experience analysis without completed design analysis.
- **Contextual Understanding**: Ensure the full user journey is understood before analyzing specific interactions.

## Engineering Rules
- **Rule 1**: Require the completed Design Analysis Report and Component Inventory.
- **Rule 2**: Require access to interactive prototypes (e.g., Figma prototypes), if available.
- **Rule 3**: Require the Engineering Brief to understand target audience and performance constraints.
- **Rule 4**: Require any specific motion or brand interaction guidelines.

## Best Practices
- Review the Design Analysis Report to identify components that require complex interactions.
- Ask designers for video references of complex animations if prototypes are not sufficient.
- Understand the target devices to determine touch vs. hover priority.

## Common Mistakes
- ❌ Starting experience analysis before the design analysis is complete.
- ❌ Relying solely on static screens without asking for interaction context.
- ❌ Ignoring performance constraints outlined in the Engineering Brief.
- ❌ Not requesting motion guidelines, leading to off-brand animations.

# Knowledge Contribution Guide

Frontend OS thrives on reusable engineering knowledge. This guide defines how and when to contribute to the knowledge base.

## When should a new document be created?
- When a new technology or tool is officially adopted.
- When a distinct, repeatable pattern is identified across multiple projects (e.g., a specific complex interaction).
- When a recurring engineering problem is solved and standardizing the solution will save time.

## When should an existing document be updated?
- When an accepted best practice changes.
- When an engineering rule is proven to be ineffective or overly restrictive.
- When a new "Common Mistake" is identified during the Review Pipeline.

## How should knowledge be extracted from completed projects?
1. During the project retrospective, identify unique components, interactions, or architectural decisions that were highly successful.
2. Strip away all client-specific branding and content.
3. Distill the solution into pure engineering rules and patterns.
4. Submit as a `Draft` document in the appropriate domain.

## How are duplicate documents avoided?
- Search the knowledge base thoroughly before creating a new document.
- Ensure your topic does not belong as a sub-section of an existing document.
- Follow the exact scope defined in the domain `README.md` files to ensure proper categorization.

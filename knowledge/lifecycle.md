# Knowledge Lifecycle

Every knowledge document in Frontend OS must follow a strict lifecycle to ensure that engineering standards are accurate, up-to-date, and reliable.

## States

### 1. Draft
- **Definition:** The document is being actively written or heavily revised.
- **Rules:** Draft documents must not be referenced by the `Principal Frontend Architect` or `Frontend Lead` for active projects.

### 2. Review
- **Definition:** The document is functionally complete and is awaiting peer review for accuracy and alignment with Frontend OS laws.
- **Rules:** Must be reviewed by at least one other engineer before approval.

### 3. Approved
- **Definition:** The document is the official standard for Frontend OS.
- **Rules:** All active projects must adhere to the rules within this document.

### 4. Deprecated
- **Definition:** The knowledge in this document is no longer the standard or has been superseded.
- **Rules:** Do not use this document for new projects. It exists only for historical reference or maintaining legacy projects.

## Workflow
`Draft` → `Review` → `Approved` → `Deprecated` (if replaced or obsolete).
